## Awaiting owner
- **Channel policy review** (S018, decides which project can succeed): the research shows that a cold start needs a burst from a large venue or an in-language community, which an English DEV account alone cannot provide (L-007, L-010, L-018; `history/research/foundation.md` §5–§6). Evidence in brief: a DEV article alone brings a median of about 1 star in its first week; where a cold-start burst's cause was found (7 of 12 cases, all 7 viral skills, the only dated language-community case), it was an HN front page, Reddit, a large X account, or the author's own post on GeekNews or linux.do. A fresh example: ninjahawk/livenerf (owner with 44 followers) had 45 stars in a week, then 744 in two days after a 894-point HN post on 2026-09-29. Options for the owner: (a) keep DEV only and design for passive discovery (a news hook, proof in the README, one-line install through skill directories, a launch-day spike that reaches per-language Trending; H-009: four of five recent English small-owner skill winners had no visible social post before their peak, though their seeds were not observed), accepting a lower expected outcome; this option becomes much stronger if a session can start within one to three days of a major model release, because small owners who shipped within days of a release gained 716–1,458 stars in week one, though counting the misses shows this held for the Opus 5.5 release and not for Fable 5 (L-023, idea #34); (b) allow the owner to post selected launches on other venues (HN rules require the owner's own words, L-001), with the developer preparing material through the outbox; (c) allow a Chinese README and Chinese-community posts (V2EX, linux.do, Juejin) for projects that fit; among small-audience owners, CJK-language repos reach 1,000 stars about 2.2 times as often as the rest, and half of the small-owner skill winners are CJK (L-019). Chinese curated weeklies accept self-recommendations through GitHub issues (ruanyf/weekly, 105k stars, 1,217 self-recommendations in 90 days; HelloGitHub, 179k), a B-class route like an awesome-list submission, but in Chinese and competitive.
