# Lessons

The program's evidence base. Entry fields: **ID**, date, statement, evidence (link), confidence (low / med / high), status (hypothesis / confirmed / refuted).
Selection (`research.md`) and launch (`launch.md`) decisions must consult this file.

## Facts
- **L-000** (2026-09-23): At program start, the owner account has 15 followers and 8 public repos, each with 0–2 stars. There is no existing audience, so reach must come from external channels. Evidence: S000 baseline. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.

_S013: HN and Reddit were removed as program channels (owner decision). L-001 and L-002 are kept as reference facts, and H-001, H-002, H-004 (HN part), and H-006 are retired._

- **L-001** (2026-09-24): Hacker News forbids generated or AI-edited text, and Show HN excludes reading material such as lists; it requires something people can try, ideally without signup. So HN posts must be written by the owner, and curated-list projects cannot launch through Show HN. Evidence: news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html and /showhn.html, fetched in S008. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-002** (2026-09-24): Reddit is unreachable from the developer's environment through every path tried: the unauthenticated API (HTTP 403, S003), the fetch tool, the built-in browser, and web search (S008). Reddit research, rule checks, and post metrics depend on the owner. Evidence: S003 and S008 logs. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.

- **L-003** (2026-09-24): DEV allows AI-generated articles only with disclosure (the `#ABotWroteThis` tag or in the text). It forbids bot or AI comments on any post, and articles whose main purpose is brand-building or clout. DEV is the only program channel the developer can publish to directly (via the API, with per-article owner approval). Evidence: dev.to/guidelines-for-ai-assisted-articles-on-dev, fetched in S012. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.

- **L-004** (2026-09-25): Since July 2026, GitHub lists a repo's stargazers (with `starred_at`) only to its admins and collaborators: REST returns 404 and GraphQL returns empty edges for anyone else. Star history of any public repo is available from `GET /repos/{owner}/{repo}/stargazers/history` (API version 2026-03-10): weekly buckets with daily counts, whose totals matched the star counts within 5 % for 500 repos. The program's own repos are unaffected. Evidence: github.blog/changelog/2026-06-30-upcoming-access-restrictions-to-public-api-endpoints-and-ui-views and 2026-09-04-new-api-endpoint-provides-privacy-safe-star-history-data; S016 tests. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-005** (2026-09-25): Base rates for repos created 2025-09-25..2026-06-27 that got ≥ 10 stars: 13.7 % reached 100 and 1.7 % reached 1,000. For owners that are users with < 100 followers (counted today): about 10 % and 0.7 %; for all other owners: about 18 % and 2.9 %. The gap is an upper bound, because hits move owners out of the small bucket. A typical cold-start repo with traction ends in the tens of stars. Evidence: `history/research/foundation.md` §4. Confidence: med (26 evenly spaced days, 18,140 repos; follower counts are current, not at launch). Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-006** (2026-09-25): Archetype matters. By normalized lift (share among winners vs among repos with 10–99 stars), agent skills are over-represented about 1.8× and AI tooling 1.25×; non-AI developer tools and playful projects (0.4× each) are under-represented. Estimated conversion from ≥ 10 to ≥ 1,000 stars: skills 3.8 % (3.4–4.2 % across 26 sampled days), AI tooling 2.7 %, curated resources 1.9 %, non-AI tools and playful projects 1.0 %. In the same problem space, the skill form outperforms the tool form: anti-slop linters stay below 40 stars while the humanizer skill has 52k. 22 % of the 3,246 winners mention Claude. Evidence: `foundation.md` §3–4, `earnstar_1/ideas.md`. Confidence: med for the ranking, low for the absolute rates (keyword proxy with 63 % agreement whose errors do not cancel across tiers). Status: the ranking is confirmed; the rates are indicative.
- **L-007** (2026-09-25): Cold starts usually need a burst borrowed from a large venue. The 49 cold-start winners in the sample (owners with < 100 followers, 1k within 90 days) gained a median 260 stars in their first week (12 had fewer than 100), with a median best day of 203. Where the ignition could be traced (7 of 12 checked, and all 7 viral skills dissected), it was one post in a large community (a Habr front page, XDA, Qiita and Zenn, a Chinese aggregator account on X, Reddit, HN) or another person's post, not the owner's audience. HN stories with ≥ 100 points were rare (4 of 49; 5 % of all winners). 30 of 49 had CJK text in the README, which is confounded with smaller follower counts. Evidence: `foundation.md` §5. Confidence: med. Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-008** (2026-09-25): README features do not separate fast from slow winners: media near the top (67 % of fast vs 71 % of slow risers), an install heading (65 % vs 67 %), and a one-line install (55 % vs 47 %) barely differ. Evidence: `foundation.md` §3. Confidence: med (winners only; this does not measure visitor conversion). Status: confirmed as a measurement.

- **L-009** (2026-09-25): The large agent-skill awesome lists cannot ignite a launch. Of five checked on 2026-09-25, three are effectively closed to submissions (ComposioHQ/awesome-claude-skills: 1,382 open PRs and none merged in 90 days; travisvn/awesome-claude-skills and composio-community/awesome-codex-skills: 0 and 1 merged). The two active ones require prior traction: VoltAgent/awesome-agent-skills (96 merges in 90 days) does not accept brand-new skills, and hesreallyhim/awesome-claude-code requires ≥ 14 days of active development or ≥ 100 stars and accepts recommendations only from humans through its web issue form, so the developer cannot submit there; only the owner could. Evidence: GitHub search and each list's CONTRIBUTING.md, S016. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-010** (2026-09-25): A DEV article alone rarely moves a repo. Among 687 articles from DEV's top-of-year lists in 24 tags (median 7 reactions) that link a repo owned by the author, the repo gained a median 1 star in the 7 days after publication (p90 12). Of 460 launches on repos with < 50 stars, 8 (1.7 %) gained ≥ 100 stars in 30 days. Reactions barely predict stars (100+ reactions: median 4). Four of the seven largest gains coincided with same-week HN stories. So DEV is a teaching and long-tail channel, not a launch engine. Evidence: `history/research/foundation.md` §6, `foundation/dev.csv`. Confidence: med (no control for how appealing each repo was). Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-011** (2026-09-25): Entering a language's GitHub Trending list can take few stars. On 2026-09-25 the daily lists showed "stars today" of 36–1,668 overall, 16–1,668 for Python, 10–1,082 TypeScript, 7–527 JavaScript, 5–1,344 Rust, 2–1,373 Go, and 0–671 Shell. GitHub's ranking is not published and is not a plain star count. A repo whose primary language GitHub detects can reach its language list with a small burst; a Markdown-only repo (no language, common for skills) competes only in the overall list. Evidence: github.com/trending pages fetched in S016. Confidence: low (one snapshot). Status: hypothesis until repeated snapshots confirm it.
- **L-012** (2026-09-25): Public SpecStory chat histories (`.specstory/history/*.md` in public GitHub repos) are a model-labelled corpus of coding-agent conversations: their agent headers name the model (e.g., `claude-opus-4-8`). Code search found 2,063 such transcripts mentioning Claude models (71,912 agent turns) on 2026-09-25, fetchable through raw.githubusercontent.com. They support measuring agent habits by model without using any account's quota, though the repos are self-selected and topics differ between them. Evidence: `history/research/earnstar_1/claudese.mjs`, S016. Confidence: med. Status: confirmed.
- **L-013** (2026-09-25): Program projects must not put "Claude", "Claude Code", or "Anthropic" in their names or logos. Anthropic's Claude Code legal page says third parties "can't use the Claude Code or Anthropic names or logos as part of your own product, feature, or company name"; accurate plain-text statements (e.g., "works with Claude Code") are allowed. Many repos ignore this, but the Constitution forbids ToS violations (§3C). The same page says subscription usage limits "assume ordinary, individual usage", so large automated evaluation runs on a subscription need care and the owner's consent. Evidence: code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance, fetched in S016; anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-014** (2026-10-01): Copying a known skill concept late earns nothing. Six "hemingway-skill" repos (2026-04 to 2026-09) have 0–4 stars; five `ponytail`-like and `adhd`-like derivatives sit at 0–2 (e.g., murrayj426/adhd-skill 2). Behavior-skill winners were first with a fresh hook and then amplified by someone else (L-007). Evidence: GitHub search, S018. Confidence: med. Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-015** (2026-10-01): Typed user messages are a poor source for ranking agent habits by keyword. In 25,653 user turns from 4,099 public SpecStory transcripts, most matches for "remove", "commit", or "placeholder" were ordinary instructions, and a third of "user" turns were injected text (summaries, skill files, diffs). Style complaints (jargon, sycophancy) were essentially absent. Ranking habits this way needs a classifier, not keywords. Evidence: `history/research/earnstar_1/corrections.mjs`, S018. Confidence: med. Status: confirmed.
- **L-016** (2026-10-01): The official Claude Code plugin directory (anthropics/claude-plugins-official, 37k stars) is not a cold-start channel. It accepts third-party plugins through a submission form with a quality and security review, but of the 199 repo owners behind its 315 entries, 196 are organizations and only 3 are individual users (two of them famous: mattpocock, 47k followers; obra, 8.8k). Evidence: its README and `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`, GitHub API, S018. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-017** (2026-10-01): Landscape searches must include the target users' languages. English-only GitHub searches missed a same-product competitor written in Japanese (minorun365/claude-code-japanese-guard), and the selection draft claimed "no existing tool"; the independent critique found it with one Japanese query. Evidence: `history/research/earnstar_1/critique.md`. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-018** (2026-10-01): Tools for a non-English developer community launch through that community's own channels (GeekNews and X for Korean, linux.do for Chinese, in-language collections such as NomaDamas/k-skill), usually by the author's own post; none of five cases used an English channel. An English DEV account cannot reach them. A single-purpose hook for one model regression stays small even for a well-placed first mover (36 stars). Evidence: `critique.md` (launch-channel research). Confidence: med. Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-019** (2026-10-01): Among owners with < 100 followers, repos with a CJK-language description convert to 1,000 stars about 2.2 times as often as the rest: their share is 16.9 % of the 10–99-star tier (n 177), 15.4 % of the 100–999 tier (n 136), and 30.6 % of the ≥ 1k sample (n 121); half of the small-owner skill winners (9 of 18) are CJK. Examples: GordenSun/GordenPPTSkill 3,150 stars (87 followers), fengshao1227/ccg-workflow 5,917 (62), lingfengQAQ/webnovel-writer 7,198 (64). The comparison within small owners removes the follower confound noted in the S016 review; the launch channels of most of these repos are unknown, and Chinese repo content needs the owner's approval (Constitution §3B). Evidence: `lab/foundation/tiers-sys.json` and `sample.json` (S018 analysis). Confidence: med. Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-020** (2026-10-01): The foundational study's window (repos created by 2026-06-27, so each had 90 days) made it blind to the newest platform waves. The largest one, deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness (created 2026-08-13; 241,349 stars on 2026-10-01; 16,927 repos with the topic `dsh-plugin`; awesome-dsh-plugin 17,499), was found only in S018 by a search in Chinese. Every selection round must also scan repos created in the last 90 days, by stars and by topic, in English and Chinese. Evidence: GitHub search, S018. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-021** (2026-10-01): Platform waves are decided in their first one to three weeks. DeepSeek Harness: of about 3,513 plugin repos created since 2026-09-01 (three weeks after launch), 10 passed 100 stars. Jev: the coding-agent winners were set within about 72 hours, and new repos fell 53 % in the second week. The program's research gate (≥ 8 hours over ≥ 2 sessions, a later-session reflection) makes it late for any wave already under way, so selection should favour predictable future launches prepared in advance (e.g., Claude Code Mods reaching general availability) or needs that do not depend on a wave. Evidence: `history/research/earnstar_1/ideas.md` #27, #33; `candidate-26-27.md`. Confidence: med. Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-022** (2026-10-01): skills.sh (run by Vercel) lists and ranks agent skills automatically from real installs: the `npx skills` CLI sends anonymous install telemetry with repo and skill identifiers for public GitHub repos, and the site shows a "Skills Leaderboard" (all time, "Trending (24h)", "Hot"; about 1.5M installs). It is a passive discovery channel that a DEV-only program can feed with a one-line install command; installs must be genuine (no self-installs, Constitution §3C). Evidence: https://skills.sh and the vercel-labs/skills README (Telemetry), fetched in S018. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-023** (2026-10-01): A model release is an amplifier that small owners can ride without an audience, if they ship within days. Launched zero to four days after a release, JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo (75 followers) gained 1,458 stars in week one, yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos (97) 1,157, Sahir619/fable-method (24) 1,125, and lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar (25) 716; notable Claude releases came every one to eight weeks in 2026. For a program whose sessions the owner schedules, this needs a session within one to three days of a release and a project prepared in advance. Evidence: `history/research/earnstar_1/ideas.md` (#34 case studies). Confidence: med (survivors; misses not counted). Status: confirmed as a measurement.
## Hypotheses (priors to test)
- **H-001** (retired, S013): A Show HN that reaches the front page yields more stars than any other single action.
- **H-002** (retired, S013): Posting on HN and Reddit within the same 48 h raises the chance of reaching GitHub Trending.
- **H-003**: A demo GIF above the fold raises the visitor-to-star conversion. _S016: among winners, fast and slow risers show media near the top equally often (L-008); visitor conversion is still untested._
- **H-004**: Disclosing AI authorship costs goodwill with some readers but earns curiosity with others; the net effect is unknown. On DEV it can be tested by comparing reactions per view. _S016: a caution: "I don't want to read what you didn't write" reached 1,056 HN points (blog.colinbreck.com, 2026-09-21), so AI-written articles must earn attention with data or technique the reader cannot get elsewhere._
- **H-005**: Curated-resource repos earn more stars per work-hour than tools. This must now be weighed against L-001. _S016: estimated conversion to 1,000 stars is 2.0 % for curated resources, 2.8 % for AI tooling, and 1.0 % for non-AI tools (L-006); stars per work-hour are still untested._
- **H-006** (retired, S013): Posting on Tuesday–Thursday at 08:00–10:00 US Eastern maximizes early votes. This is a widely repeated heuristic with no source verified.
- **H-008** (S018): A platform launch (e.g., Claude Code "Mods" reaching general availability) creates search demand that GitHub search, topics, and lists capture without social amplification, so a ready, well-named project at launch can grow on discovery alone. Precedent: after the Skills launch (2025-10-16) community lists grew to 15k (travisvn) and 76k (ComposioHQ) stars, after the MCP launch punkpeye/awesome-mcp-servers grew to 96k; their traffic sources are not observable. Status: hypothesis.
- **H-009** (S018): Without a social venue, a launch can still ignite through passive discovery if it combines a news hook, proof in the README (benchmark numbers, before/after visuals), one-line install through skill directories (e.g., skills.sh), and a concentrated launch-day spike that reaches a per-language Trending list (L-011). Evidence: of five recent English skill winners by owners with < 100 followers (miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop 4,201; Ryze-AI-Adgent/open-seo-mcp-skills 2,947; Sahir619/fable-method 2,299; coldteadotai/pr-lens 1,783; Spielewoy/autoprompt-skill 1,298), four had no social post found before their peak (Reddit and X could not be searched), Trending appeared on or after the peak day, and no DEV article played a role (S018 subagent; `history/research/earnstar_1/ideas.md`). Status: hypothesis; the seeds of the first spikes were not observed.
- **H-007**: A technical DEV article about how a project was built drives more long-tail stars (through tags and search) than a social post from a new account. _S016: a DEV article alone brings a median of 1 star in its first week (L-010); the long-tail part is still untested._
