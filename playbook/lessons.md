# Lessons

The program's evidence base. Entry fields: **ID**, date, statement, evidence (link), confidence (low / med / high), status (hypothesis / confirmed / refuted).
Selection (`research.md`) and launch (`launch.md`) decisions must consult this file.

## Facts
- **L-000** (2026-09-23): At program start, the owner account has 15 followers and 8 public repos, each with 0–2 stars. There is no existing audience, so reach must come from external channels. Evidence: S000 baseline. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.

_S013: HN and Reddit were removed as program channels (owner decision). L-001 and L-002 are kept as reference facts, and H-001, H-002, H-004 (HN part), and H-006 are retired._

- **L-001** (2026-09-24): Hacker News forbids generated or AI-edited text, and Show HN excludes reading material such as lists; it requires something people can try, ideally without signup. So HN posts must be written by the owner, and curated-list projects cannot launch through Show HN. Evidence: news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html and /showhn.html, fetched in S008. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-002** (2026-09-24): Reddit is unreachable from the developer's environment through every path tried: the unauthenticated API (HTTP 403, S003), the fetch tool, the built-in browser, and web search (S008). Reddit research, rule checks, and post metrics depend on the owner. Evidence: S003 and S008 logs. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.

- **L-003** (2026-09-24): DEV allows AI-generated articles only with disclosure (the `#ABotWroteThis` tag or in the text). It forbids bot or AI comments on any post, and articles whose main purpose is brand-building or clout. DEV is the only program channel the developer can publish to directly (via the API, with per-article owner approval). Evidence: dev.to/guidelines-for-ai-assisted-articles-on-dev, fetched in S012. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.

- **L-004** (2026-09-25): Since July 2026, GitHub lists a repo's stargazers (with `starred_at`) only to its admins and collaborators: REST returns 404 and GraphQL returns empty edges for anyone else. Star history of any public repo is available from `GET /repos/{owner}/{repo}/stargazers/history` (API version 2026-03-10): weekly buckets with daily counts, whose totals matched the star counts within 5 % for 500 repos. The program's own repos are unaffected. Evidence: github.blog/changelog/2026-06-30-upcoming-access-restrictions-to-public-api-endpoints-and-ui-views and 2026-09-04-new-api-endpoint-provides-privacy-safe-star-history-data; S016 tests. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-005** (2026-09-25): Base rates for repos created 2025-09-25..2026-06-27 that got ≥ 10 stars: 13.7 % reached 100 and 1.7 % reached 1,000. For owners that are users with < 100 followers: 10.2 % and 0.57 %; for all other owners: 22.4 % and 4.4 %. A typical cold-start repo with traction ends in the tens of stars. Evidence: `history/research/foundation.md` §4. Confidence: med (tier mixes come from 6 random days). Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-006** (2026-09-25): Archetype matters. Estimated conversion from ≥ 10 to ≥ 1,000 stars: agent skills 4.2 %, AI-agent tooling and apps 2.8 %, curated resources 2.0 %, non-AI developer tools 1.0 %, playful projects 0.8 %. In the same problem space, the skill form outperforms the tool form: anti-slop linters stay below 40 stars while the humanizer skill has 52k. 22 % of the 3,246 winners mention Claude. Evidence: `foundation.md` §3–4, `earnstar_1/ideas.md`. Confidence: med (keyword proxy with 63 % agreement). Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-007** (2026-09-25): Cold starts need a burst borrowed from a large venue. The 49 cold-start winners in the sample (owners with < 100 followers, 1k within 90 days) gained a median 260 stars in their first week, with a best day of 203. Their ignition was one post in a large community (a Habr front page, XDA, Qiita and Zenn, a Chinese aggregator account on X, Reddit), not the owner's audience. HN stories with ≥ 100 points were rare (4 of 49; 5 % of all winners). 30 of 49 had Chinese text in the README. Evidence: `foundation.md` §5. Confidence: med. Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-008** (2026-09-25): README features are table stakes among winners, not differentiators: media near the top (67 % of fast vs 71 % of slow risers), an install heading (65 % vs 67 %), and a one-line install (55 % vs 47 %) barely differ. Evidence: `foundation.md` §3. Confidence: med (winners only; this does not measure visitor conversion). Status: confirmed as a measurement.

- **L-009** (2026-09-25): The large agent-skill awesome lists cannot ignite a launch. Of five checked on 2026-09-25, three are effectively closed to submissions (ComposioHQ/awesome-claude-skills: 1,382 open PRs and none merged in 90 days; travisvn/awesome-claude-skills and composio-community/awesome-codex-skills: 0 and 1 merged). The two active ones require prior traction: VoltAgent/awesome-agent-skills (96 merges in 90 days) does not accept brand-new skills, and hesreallyhim/awesome-claude-code requires ≥ 14 days of active development or ≥ 100 stars and accepts recommendations only from humans through its web issue form, so the developer cannot submit there; only the owner could. Evidence: GitHub search and each list's CONTRIBUTING.md, S016. Confidence: high. Status: confirmed.
- **L-010** (2026-09-25): A DEV article alone rarely moves a repo. Among 687 of DEV's most-reacted articles of the past year (24 tags) that link a repo owned by the author, the repo gained a median 1 star in the 7 days after publication (p75 4, p90 10); for repos with < 50 stars beforehand, 2 % reached 100 stars within 30 days. Reactions barely predict stars (100+ reactions: median 4). The largest gains coincided with same-week HN stories. So DEV is a teaching and long-tail channel, not a launch engine. Evidence: `history/research/foundation.md` §6, `foundation/dev.csv`. Confidence: med (top articles only; typical articles do worse). Status: confirmed as a measurement.
- **L-011** (2026-09-25): Entering a language's GitHub Trending list can take few stars. On 2026-09-25 the daily lists showed "stars today" of 36–1,668 overall, 16–1,668 for Python, 10–1,082 TypeScript, 7–527 JavaScript, 5–1,344 Rust, 2–1,373 Go, and 0–671 Shell. GitHub's ranking is not published and is not a plain star count. A repo whose primary language GitHub detects can reach its language list with a small burst; a Markdown-only repo (no language, common for skills) competes only in the overall list. Evidence: github.com/trending pages fetched in S016. Confidence: low (one snapshot). Status: hypothesis until repeated snapshots confirm it.
## Hypotheses (priors to test)
- **H-001** (retired, S013): A Show HN that reaches the front page yields more stars than any other single action.
- **H-002** (retired, S013): Posting on HN and Reddit within the same 48 h raises the chance of reaching GitHub Trending.
- **H-003**: A demo GIF above the fold raises the visitor-to-star conversion. _S016: among winners, fast and slow risers show media near the top equally often (L-008); visitor conversion is still untested._
- **H-004**: Disclosing AI authorship costs goodwill with some readers but earns curiosity with others; the net effect is unknown. On DEV it can be tested by comparing reactions per view. _S016: a caution: "I don't want to read what you didn't write" reached 1,056 HN points (blog.colinbreck.com, 2026-09-21), so AI-written articles must earn attention with data or technique the reader cannot get elsewhere._
- **H-005**: Curated-resource repos earn more stars per work-hour than tools. This must now be weighed against L-001. _S016: estimated conversion to 1,000 stars is 2.0 % for curated resources, 2.8 % for AI tooling, and 1.0 % for non-AI tools (L-006); stars per work-hour are still untested._
- **H-006** (retired, S013): Posting on Tuesday–Thursday at 08:00–10:00 US Eastern maximizes early votes. This is a widely repeated heuristic with no source verified.
- **H-007**: A technical DEV article about how a project was built drives more long-tail stars (through tags and search) than a social post from a new account. _S016: a DEV article alone brings a median of 1 star in its first week (L-010); the long-tail part is still untested._
